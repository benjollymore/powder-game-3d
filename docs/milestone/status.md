## Integrated since the interruption

- **File shortcuts and modifier hygiene.** Cmd/Ctrl+S saves a named build without a chooser, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S always asks for a path, Cmd/Ctrl+O enters the guarded Open workflow. Plain sandbox keys require no modifier, so Cmd/Ctrl+P/N/X/B no longer pause, step or change tools; Undo/Redo from a focused text field stays in that field. An independent review found that a queued Save As could be silently replaced by a shortcut Open, leaving the unsaved-build guard stuck so Reset, Empty, Open and close did nothing; file actions now reject rather than replace a queued dialog, with visible feedback, and the guard leaves its saving state whenever no chooser can resolve it. A physical-key fallback for non-Latin layouts was added, then narrowed after review showed it hijacked Colemak/Dvorak/QWERTZ shortcuts. CPU: 24 shortcut and 10 guard-queue checks. GPU: 20 shortcut checks and 32 protection checks at each grid size. [Evidence](final-integrated/).
- **Ordinary thin liquid.** Paused isolated or one-cell-thick liquid was pickable but invisible. Nonfalling liquid with open neighbours on both sides of an axis now renders as an amount-aware, gravity-aligned slab. Review found the nominal-full fast path required amount exactly 200 while the hydro kernel compresses settled cells, so live reservoirs would have paid the rejected classifier cost; the contract is now that any interval already covered by a regular liquid segment uses the bulk path regardless of amount, and the cost fixture gained a hydro-compressed variant. Falling non-spray thin liquid remains a documented omission. Coordinator reruns: 138 geometry, 119 interface, 28 control, 145/66/61/102 overflow and liquid checks. [ordinary-liquid.md](ordinary-liquid.md).
- **Powder heap rings.** Concentric bands on sand cones came from the curvature-darkening Laplacian saturating on planar slopes and from the one-cell share of the shading normal, not from geometry. Smoothed powders now shade from level-1/level-2 gradients; walls render byte-identically and physical bytes are unchanged. Lit ripple fell to roughly a quarter on 0.35 and 0.6 slopes. Review found the first version gave section caps, embedded cameras and coarse hits of smoothed materials a constant diagonal normal, reversing the earlier section-normal fix; the overrides now apply to the smoothed normal too, the surface suite cuts Sand and Plant as well as Wall (99 checks), and the ripple suite records wall-adjacent tilt and wood/plant deltas (51 checks). Sand within 2-4 cells of a wall gains up to 6 degrees of tilt; recorded, not faded. [powder-ripple.md](powder-ripple.md).
- **Gravity and plane-constraint mechanics.** The [declared experiment](mechanics-experiment-plan.md) is implemented and evaluated in [mechanics-motion.md](mechanics-motion.md). Its original claim that a flat plane discriminates PIC from APIC was wrong for a purely translating cluster, so the lower cluster now rotates at 5 rad/s. ## Architectural position

Keep the GPU material sandbox and editor as a useful product foundation. The existing cellular model alone is not a sufficient long-term architecture for all requested ambitions. General momentum, conservative heat/phase change, stress and moving rigid assemblies require explicit state and coupling contracts. The [architecture assessment](architecture-feasibility.md), [thermal reference](thermal-feasibility.md), and [momentum reference](momentum-feasibility.md) distinguish verified narrow properties from missing solver features.

[Current architectural implications](architecture-progress.md) connect the completed experiments to the direction: retain editor contracts and a usable cellular baseline while evaluating richer authoritative state behind them.
